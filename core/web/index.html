Keyboard shortcuts and tips

Creating: 768 px ≈ 4 min, 1024 px ≈ 6.5 min (base M4 Mac; your hardware may differ). Editing takes several times longer (the photo is part of the computation), so its scale is smaller: 512 / 640 / 768 / 1024 px. While one job is running, the rest wait in the queue.
