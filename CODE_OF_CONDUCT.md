# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

## Our pledge

We want participation in ImageLoom to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure about someone's intent.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused and on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, doxxing, or sexualized language and imagery directed at people.
- Posting other people's private information, or generated images depicting real people without their consent.
- Using the project's spaces to share illegal content, or content that sexualizes minors (such reports are passed to the authorities).
- Spam and unsolicited promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider harmful. Report problems privately through the repository's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form (choose "other") or by contacting the maintainers on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
